Roblox

Roblox is a global platform where people can play, create, and share games with others.

What Roblox offers

Roblox is built around user-created experiences. Players can explore different worlds, play with friends, and make their own games.

Some parts of Roblox include:

  • Player-created games and experiences
  • Avatars and custom items
  • Social features for playing with friends
  • Tools for creating interactive worlds

How people play

Roblox has many kinds of experiences, including adventure, role-playing, racing, building, and obstacle-course games.
